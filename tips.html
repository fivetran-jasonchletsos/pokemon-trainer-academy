Trainer Tips

Great trainers are smart, kind, and careful. Here is how to be one.

Catching tips

  • Weaken first. In the games, a Pokemon with low HP is easier to catch. Be gentle so you do not Knock it Out.
  • Use a status condition. Putting a wild Pokemon to sleep or paralyzing it can help your chances.
  • Stock up. Always carry plenty of Poke Balls, and try different kinds. Some work better in certain places.
  • Be patient. If a Pokemon breaks out of the ball, just try again. Some take a few tries.
  • Explore everywhere. Caves, water, tall grass, and different times of day have different Pokemon.
  • Fill your team with variety. Try to bring different types so you are ready for anything. Use our Dream Team page to practice!

Being a good sport

  • Say "good luck" before a battle and "good game" afterward, whether you win or lose.
  • Take turns, play by the rules, and never cheat.
  • Do not brag when you win. Do not get upset when you lose. Every loss helps you learn.
  • Be kind to new players. Everyone was a beginner once, so offer to teach them.
  • Trade fairly and make sure both people are happy. Do not pressure anyone to swap their favorite cards.
  • Take care of shared cards and games. Say thank you.

Staying safe online

Pokemon games and websites are fun, and the internet is a big place. Follow these trainer rules.

  • Never share personal information. Do not tell anyone online your real name, address, school, phone number, passwords, or photos. Use a nickname that does not tell who you are.
  • Ask a grown-up before downloading. Only get games and apps from places your grown-up says are safe.
  • Ask a grown-up before buying. That includes games, in-game items, and card packs. Never use a card or account without permission.
  • Keep passwords secret. Share them only with your parents or guardians.
  • Be careful with strangers. If someone you do not know sends you a message or asks to meet, do not answer. Tell a grown-up right away.
  • Watch out for tricks. If something promises free items, free money, or rare cards, it is probably a trick. Ask a grown-up.
  • Tell a grown-up if something feels wrong. You will never be in trouble for asking for help.

A trainer pledge

I will be kind. I will be careful. I will keep learning. I will share the fun with my friends.
