The Video Games

In the main Pokemon video games, you become a trainer, travel around a region, catch Pokemon, and challenge strong trainers. Each new generation visits a new region with new Pokemon. At the start, you choose one of three starter Pokemon: a Grass type, a Fire type, and a Water type.

Kanto

Generation 1

The very first adventure. 151 Pokemon to discover, and the journey that started it all.

Starter Pokemon: Bulbasaur (Grass), Charmander (Fire), Squirtle (Water)

Main games: Red, Blue, and Yellow. Remakes: FireRed and LeafGreen, and Let's Go, Pikachu! and Let's Go, Eevee!.

Johto

Generation 2

Next door to Kanto. Introduced day and night, and Pokemon eggs for breeding.

Starter Pokemon: Chikorita (Grass), Cyndaquil (Fire), Totodile (Water)

Main games: Gold, Silver, and Crystal. Remakes: HeartGold and SoulSilver.

Hoenn

Generation 3

A tropical region with lots of water. Introduced Abilities and Double Battles.

Starter Pokemon: Treecko (Grass), Torchic (Fire), Mudkip (Water)

Main games: Ruby, Sapphire, and Emerald. Remakes: Omega Ruby and Alpha Sapphire.

Sinnoh

Generation 4

A snowy mountain region. Introduced online trading and battling.

Starter Pokemon: Turtwig (Grass), Chimchar (Fire), Piplup (Water)

Main games: Diamond, Pearl, and Platinum. Remakes: Brilliant Diamond and Shining Pearl. Also Pokemon Legends: Arceus, which takes place long ago in the same land, called Hisui.

Unova

Generation 5

A big-city region inspired by New York. The first games where you meet almost all-new Pokemon.

Starter Pokemon: Snivy (Grass), Tepig (Fire), Oshawott (Water)

Main games: Black and White, and Black 2 and White 2.

Kalos

Generation 6

A region inspired by France. The first games in full 3D, and the home of Mega Evolution and the Fairy type.

Starter Pokemon: Chespin (Grass), Fennekin (Fire), Froakie (Water)

Main games: X and Y.

Alola

Generation 7

A sunny island region inspired by Hawaii. Introduced Z-Moves and Alolan forms.

Starter Pokemon: Rowlet (Grass), Litten (Fire), Popplio (Water)

Main games: Sun and Moon, and Ultra Sun and Ultra Moon.

Galar

Generation 8

A region inspired by Great Britain. Introduced Dynamax and the Wild Area.

Starter Pokemon: Grookey (Grass), Scorbunny (Fire), Sobble (Water)

Main games: Sword and Shield.

Paldea

Generation 9

A region inspired by Spain and Portugal. The first fully open-world main games, with Terastallizing.

Starter Pokemon: Sprigatito (Grass), Fuecoco (Fire), Quaxly (Water)

Main games: Scarlet and Violet.

More Pokemon games

  • Pokemon GO. A mobile game where you walk around in the real world to find and catch Pokemon. Always have a grown-up with you when you play outside.
  • Pokemon Trading Card Game Pocket. A phone game for collecting and battling with digital cards.
  • Pokemon Mystery Dungeon. You become a Pokemon and explore mysterious dungeons.
  • Pokemon Snap games. A photography adventure where you take pictures of Pokemon in the wild.
  • Pokemon UNITE. A team battle game where trainers work together.

Always ask a grown-up before you download, buy, or play any game online.
