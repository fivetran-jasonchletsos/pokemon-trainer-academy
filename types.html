The whole chart

Rows are the attacking type. Columns are the defending type. A green 2 means super effective, a red 1/2 means not very effective, and a black 0 means no effect. Blank means normal damage. Scroll sideways on a small screen.

Remember

A Pokemon with two types gets both matchups multiplied together. A move that is super effective against one of its types and not very effective against the other ends up as normal damage. Some Pokemon are weak to a type twice and take four times damage!
